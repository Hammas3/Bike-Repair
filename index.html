Educational Tips

Bike Maintenance Tips

Simple habits to keep your motorcycle in healthy operating condition.

01

Check Tire Condition

Regularly inspect tire tread depth and maintain correct air pressure for stable handling and safety.

02

Maintain Chain Tension

Keep the drive chain properly cleaned, lubricated, and tensioned to prevent premature wear.

03

Listen to Engine Sounds

Pay attention to any unusual knocking, rattling, or whining noises coming from the engine block.

04

Monitor Fluid Levels

Keep a close eye on engine oil and brake fluid levels to avoid internal component damage.

05

Check Brake Performance

Test your front and rear brakes periodically for responsiveness and firm lever/pedal feedback.

06

Maintain Electrical Connections

Ensure battery terminals are clean and lights, indicators, and horn operate reliably.

07

Address Issues Early

Resolve minor performance hesitations before they develop into major roadside breakdowns.

08

Follow Recommendations

Adhere to your motorcycle manufacturer's maintenance schedule for oil changes and tune-ups.
